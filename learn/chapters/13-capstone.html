🎯 Key takeaways

  • A new capability is mostly design: the right primitive, a precise schema, stable errors.
  • Writes need defense in depth: scopes on the server, approval in the host, idempotency on retries.
  • Ship it with unit, integration and end-to-end tests before it reaches a real store.
